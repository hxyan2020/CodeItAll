    FAQ

    Questions about Website Dissect

    What can Website Dissect access?

    Only publicly fetchable HTML. We map structure and explain it for learning. Private dashboards, paywalls, and authenticated apps stay out of reach.

    Is this for beginners learning HTML?

    Yes. It helps beginners and intermediate learners connect textbook HTML to real public pages—navigation, forms, and section structure.

    Can I rebuild a simpler version of a page?

    You can ask for a learner-friendly skeleton that mirrors structure. Do not copy brand assets or proprietary content—use it as a study aid.

    Does Dissect bypass login walls?

    No. If content requires login or payment, we note what is missing instead of attempting unauthorized access.
