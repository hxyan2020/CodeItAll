    Questions about Career Booster

    What is Career Booster?

    It generates practice project packs from your industry, role, and language choices so you train on work-like tasks—not toy tutorials alone.

    Which roles are supported?

    Hints cover software and frontend/backend engineers, data analysts, quant researchers, product analysts, DevOps, and more—or type your own role.

    Do projects open in the interactive lab?

    Yes. Generated packs open in the matching language lab with the same run workflow as the fun course tracks.

    Is this a job guarantee or interview prep course?

    Career Lab packs are educational simulations for skill practice. They help you build fluency for interviews and portfolios; they are not hiring offers.
